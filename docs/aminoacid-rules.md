	•	Peptide length information
2-9 aa: very short bioactive peptides; cell-cell communication, neuropeptides, minimal inhibitor cores
10-50 aa: primary functional window; anti-bacterial, anti-fungal, anti-cancer, anti-parasitic, anti-viral, toxic, drug-delivery (CPP), immunological, inhibitor
15-30 aa: signal peptide
> 50 aa: metabolic hormones, structural peptides, predominantly non-functional in most databases
 > 100 aa: considered a protein, not a peptide; excluded from peptide benchmarks
	•	Criterion 2 — Net charge at pH 7
	•	< 0 (anionic): only 6% of validated AMPs are anionic; non-functional or highly specific receptor-binding peptides
	•	0 to +1: near-neutral; cell-cell communication, metabolic hormones, immunological epitopes, inhibitor peptides
	•	+1 to +3: weak cationic; anti-viral, anti-cancer, immunological, metabolic
	•	+2 to +5: moderate cationic; core AMP window-anti-bacterial, anti-fungal, anti-parasitic, toxic
	•	+3 to +9: strongly cationic; drug-delivery (CPP)-most CPPs contain > 5 positive residues
